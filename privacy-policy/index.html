Privacy Policy

This notice explains the current data practices of Void Plane and its official information website.

Developer and operator: ARENAGO. Contact: arenagoofficial@gmail.com. Effective date: 13 November 2026.

1. Information handled

  • Sign-in information. Google and Supabase process the information needed to authenticate your account. This may include your account identifier, email address and basic Google profile information authorized during sign-in. The game uses a Supabase account identifier and session credentials to identify the signed-in account. ARENAGO does not receive your Google password.
  • Game information. Your chosen in-game name, character selection, inventory, equipment, currencies, account skills and expedition progress are used to run and save the game. The current game saves progress on your device. Multiplayer also exchanges relevant player/profile and gameplay state with the game server and may store expedition/checkpoint records there.
  • Technical information. Authentication, hosting and multiplayer infrastructure handle connection information, such as IP addresses, request/connection times and operational or diagnostic records needed to provide and protect their services.
  • Support requests. If you email ARENAGO, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. Please do not send passwords, login codes or payment details.

2. Why information is used

Information is used to sign you in, keep local progress associated with the selected account, provide multiplayer gameplay and reconnect/checkpoint features, respond to support or deletion requests, and investigate failures or misuse. In-game names, characters and shared gameplay state can be visible to other players in your multiplayer session.

The current game does not integrate a third-party advertising SDK. Optional advertising features, if introduced, may require an updated notice and additional choices before they are used.

3. Service providers and sharing

Google provides Google account sign-in; Supabase provides the authentication service; multiplayer servers and connection/relay providers deliver online play; GitHub Pages hosts this website. These providers may process information outside your country under their own privacy notices. Information is shared as needed to provide those functions, respond to your requests, or comply with applicable legal requirements. ARENAGO does not sell your personal information.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Supabase Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Unity Privacy Policy — the game is built with Unity; applicable engine/platform services are governed by their notices.

4. Storage, security and retention

Game progress is stored locally and relevant multiplayer checkpoint/session records may be stored on the game server. Authentication and account-deletion requests use HTTPS. Local session credentials use supported platform protection, such as Android Keystore or Windows DPAPI.

Local progress remains until it is replaced, reset or deleted through the game's storage/account flows or device management. Sign-in account information is kept while the account is maintained, until deletion is completed. Separately stored multiplayer records require their own deletion review. Provider-managed operational, security and legal records may be retained under the provider's policies and applicable requirements. When handling a deletion request, ARENAGO will explain any information that must be retained and the applicable retention period or criteria.

5. Account and data deletion

You can request deletion from the game's Settings → Delete Account confirmation, or contact ARENAGO without installing or signing into the game using the account-deletion instructions.

The in-game flow deletes the Void Plane sign-in account held by Supabase and the matching on-device profile after confirmation. It does not delete your Google/Gmail account. Deleting the sign-in account does not by itself guarantee deletion of all separately stored multiplayer checkpoints, copies on other devices, or provider-managed logs. Contact ARENAGO for a review/removal request covering remaining gameplay records. We may need to verify account ownership before acting.

6. Your choices and contact

You can choose not to start Google sign-in, sign out through the game, or request account/data deletion. Google account permissions can also be reviewed in your Google Account. Reading/acknowledging this policy in the game does not replace Google's authorization screen or any additional consent required for a separate feature.

If you have privacy questions, requests concerning a child's information, or an access/correction/deletion request, email arenagoofficial@gmail.com. The information needed to verify and fulfil a request depends on its scope.

7. Website and policy changes

This information website has no account form or advertising/analytics script added by ARENAGO. GitHub, your browser and linked services may process normal hosting/connection information. This policy may be updated as the game and its data practices change; the effective date above identifies the current notice.
