Request account deletion

You can request deletion in the game or by email. The email route is available without signing into or reinstalling Void Plane.

Option 1 — In the game

  1. Sign into the Void Plane account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings, then select Delete Account.
  3. Read the warning and choose YES to confirm. Choose NO to keep the account.

After a confirmed successful deletion, this removes the Void Plane sign-in account from Supabase and the matching profile on that device, including local character/progress, inventory/equipment, storage, currencies and account-skill information for that profile. Other local account profiles are not selected for deletion by this action.

Option 2 — Contact ARENAGO

Email arenagoofficial@gmail.com with the subject Void Plane account deletion request.

  • Use the email associated with your Void Plane Google sign-in where possible.
  • State that you want your Void Plane account and associated data deleted.
  • Include your in-game name, if known, and whether the request should also cover multiplayer/checkpoint records or copies on other devices.

Do not send your Google password, access tokens, login codes or payment details. ARENAGO may request limited information needed to verify ownership and locate the relevant records. Request handling depends on verification and scope; we will explain any additional information, processing steps or retention exceptions when responding.

What is retained or handled separately?

The in-game deletion flow does not yet guarantee automatic removal of separately stored multiplayer checkpoint/session records. Request a review of these records by email. Local copies on other devices and provider-managed operational/security logs also require separate handling where applicable. Any information that must be retained for a legitimate security or legal purpose, and its retention period or criteria, will be explained in the response to your request.

This deletes your Void Plane account; it does not delete your Google or Gmail account. Signing in again after deletion can create a new Void Plane sign-in account.
